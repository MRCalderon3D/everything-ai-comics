# Color script: The Night Ferry

Color mode: `monochrome`

The comic has no color to plan in ISS_001. This file tracks how much of each
page is black, where the white is allowed, and when the one spot color may
appear.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Ink black | `#000000` | Night, water, shadow, lettering. |
| Paper white | `#FFFFFF` | Lamp light, moonlight on wet steel, balloons, the searchlight. |

## Spot colors

| Name | Hex | The one thing it marks | First appears |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signal red | `#C0392B` | The light of a fired flare. | Not in ISS_001. |

## Scene by scene

| Scene | Time of day | Light source | Dominant values | What changes from the scene before |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SC_001_01 | Night | Shielded wheelhouse lamp, moon, and a searchlight in the last panel. | Mostly black. White only where a light source reaches. | First scene. |

## Page by page

| Page | Panels | Note |
|---|---|---|
| PG_001_001 | 1 | The ferry is the only white shape on the page. The lamp is a small white window. |
| PG_001_001 | 2, 4 | Lamp light from above. Mara's face is half white, half black. |
| PG_001_002 | 1, 2 | No lamp on deck. Moonlight gives thin white edges on the ladder, the gun, and the tarpaulin. |
| PG_001_002 | 3 | Oskar's face is the largest white shape on the page. |
| PG_001_002 | 4 | The searchlight is a hard white wedge at screen right. It does not reach the deck yet. |

## Progression

Page 1 is the darkest: one small light in a black river. Page 2 gives up the
lamp and gets darker still until the tarpaulin lifts, and the boy's face
becomes the brightest thing in the issue. The searchlight in the last panel is
brighter than the lamp ever was, and it comes from behind her.

## Continuity notes

- The cut on Oskar's left hand is black, and so is the stain on his left cuff. Both stay for the whole issue.
- Whether the wheelhouse lamp is still lit while Mara is on deck has not been decided. It is listed as an open issue in `continuity/ISS_001.final-state.yaml`.
