{{/if}}WHAT COUNTS AS A FINDING
- A finding is something a reader would notice, or something that breaks identity, continuity, the story beat, the exact lettering text, or the style of this comic. If nobody reading the page would see it and nothing later depends on it, it is not a finding.
- Do not report a difference of degree the plan does not quantify. "Leans forward" drawn as a slight lean is what was planned; so is "smiles" drawn as a small smile.
- Do not report what the plan leaves open: the side an unplanned accessory sits on, a background detail nobody specified, the exact shape of a pose.
- Blocking is for: a wrong, ambiguous or duplicated identity; wrong or missing text; a story action that is missing or done by the wrong character; a position, a level or a causality that cannot be; a physical state, an object or a custody that contradicts the ledger; a style that has left the look of this comic. If the image does not establish who acts, that is blocking even when a caption names them.
- Everything else that is worth telling the person is minor.
- A minor finding does not trigger a repair. The page passes with it listed as a note, and the person decides whether to ask for a correction. A blocking finding does trigger a repair, which costs minutes and can disturb what was right. So the severity has to be honest in both directions: do not call a defect blocking to get it fixed, and do not call a real break minor to let the page through.
- A check is "fail" only when it has a blocking finding. A check with only a minor finding stays "pass", and its evidence mentions the note.
- A gate fails only when a blocking finding of its kind is open: style_fidelity for the style, narrative_continuity for everything else.
