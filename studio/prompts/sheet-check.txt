WHAT TO COMPARE
- The face: shape, eyes, nose, mouth, apparent age.
- The hair and facial hair: colour, length, cut.
- The build and the proportions.
- Every mark, scar, tattoo, and accessory that identifies the character, and the side of the body it is on.

The new sheet may show other views, expressions, poses, or another outfit than the master. That is what it is for, and it is not a finding. A difference is a finding only when it makes the person look like someone else, or moves or loses a mark.
