# Preview test scene

The one page every preset draws for its sample image. It stays the same for
all looks so that differences in the samples come from the look, not the
content. Do not change it for one preset; if it changes, every sample has to
be drawn again.

## Page

- One portrait comic page, 2:3, three panels.
- Layout: panel 1 runs the full width of the top half; panels 2 and 3 sit
  side by side in the bottom half, panel 2 on the left.
- The look decides borders, gutters, line, tone, color, and lettering style.
  The layout above stays the same even when a preset's `Panels` line
  describes another one (a strip tier, a yonkoma column, a scroll), so the
  samples stay comparable.
- Reading order left to right, top to bottom.

## Character

ODILE MARCH, the only character on the page. An invented adult, not based on
any real person.

- Woman in her early forties, medium height, sturdy build.
- Round face, thick straight eyebrows, a small mole above the left corner of
  her mouth.
- Dark hair cut to the jaw, with a short fringe.
- Long ochre wool coat with three large dark buttons, a dark green scarf, a
  brown leather bag on a strap across her body.
- In monochrome looks the coat reads as a light-mid value, the scarf and hair
  as dark values.

## Location

A narrow stone footbridge over a town canal at dusk. A single iron lamp post
stands at the middle of the bridge, already lit. Behind the bridge, a row of
narrow three-storey houses with a few warm lit windows. Dark water under the
arch, with reflections of the lamp and the windows. The sky is deep blue at
the top and paler near the rooftops.

Light: the warm lamp is the key light on Odile in every panel. The cool dusk
sky fills the shadows.

## Prop

A small wooden box, about the size of a shoebox, tied with a cross of pale
string and a blank paper tag. Odile carries it in every panel.

## Panels

### Panel 1 (wide)

Establishing view of the bridge, the houses, and the canal, eye level, from
the canal bank. Odile walks across the bridge from left to right, small in the
frame but readable, the box held under her right arm. The lamp is ahead of
her.

Caption, top left corner: `The last delivery of the day.`

### Panel 2 (close-up, hands)

Close-up of Odile's two hands holding the box in front of her coat, under the
lamp. Her left thumb lifts the blank paper tag. Fingers, knuckles, and the
crossing string are clear. The coat buttons and the edge of the scarf are at
the top of the frame.

No text.

### Panel 3 (close-up, face)

Odile's head and shoulders, three-quarter view facing left, lit warm from the
lamp on one side and cool from the sky on the other. She looks up at
something out of frame and gives a small, tired smile. The corner of the box
shows at the bottom of the frame.

Speech balloon, from Odile, tail to her mouth: `Right on time.`

## What the page tests

| Panel | Exercises |
|---|---|
| 1 | Line on architecture and a full figure, blacks or tone in the water and the arch, color of dusk and lamp light, a caption. |
| 2 | Hands and fingers, a prop with string and tag, small detail, warm key light. |
| 3 | Face and expression, the character's fixed features (mole, fringe, eyebrows), two-color lighting, a speech balloon. |

Lettering must match the two strings above exactly, with no other text on
the page. A tag with writing on it, a sign on a house, or a sound effect
counts as a fault in the sample.
