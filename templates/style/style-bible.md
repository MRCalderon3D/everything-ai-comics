# Style bible

The visual language of this comic. `/style-bible` fills this file, the art
director approves it, and every page is reviewed against it. Write rules a
reviewer can check on a rendered page. "Moody" is not checkable; "shadows are
flat black shapes with hard edges" is.

Status: draft
